Multi-Transport Traveling Salesman

Compare total distance, journey time, and cost across ICE car, EV car, coach, train, and flight for a tour visiting your chosen destinations.

1. Destinations

Names are looked up in a built-in gazetteer, then (optionally) OpenStreetMap Nominatim or Google Geocoding. If a name can't be resolved you'll be prompted for lat,lon.

    1b. Group travel (optional)

    Add one home city per person. The solver tries every destination as the meeting point and picks the one minimising (everyone's travel to meet) + (the joint tour from there) per mode.

      2. Schedule (optional but enables date-aware results)

      Per-city overrides (stay length, pinned date) appear next to each destination above once you've added them. Pins require a Start date.

      3. Rail policy (optional)

      Train (regular per-km ticket pricing) is always shown. Tick the Interrail box to also show an Interrail row that uses pass pricing (per-leg reservation fees + a Global Pass cost added once for the whole trip) and enforces the 33-country Interrail area. "Exclude Eurostar" affects only the Interrail / Night-train rows. "Night trains: only" hides Train and Interrail and leaves the slower Night-train mode (with sleeper supplement).

      4. What to minimise & currency

      Cache lives in your browser's localStorage and lasts 24 hours. Speeds up repeated runs and reduces Nominatim/Google/Amadeus/SerpAPI calls.

      5. Live data (optional)

      Google Maps (geocoding + driving/transit routing)

      Loads the official Google Maps JS SDK with your key. The browser calls Geocoder and DistanceMatrixService directly. Charges hit your Google billing account; check your quotas.

      Amadeus Self-Service (recommended free tier)

      Sign up at developers.amadeus.com for a free key (2000 calls/month). The browser calls Amadeus directly; their CORS policy is permissive but check your DevTools console if the call is blocked. Each city is mapped to its nearest IATA airport automatically.

      Google Flights prices (via SerpAPI)

      Google has no public Flights API; SerpAPI's google_flights engine is the standard bridge. SerpAPI doesn't allow direct browser calls (no CORS), so requests usually need a small proxy. If the call fails the program falls back to per-km defaults.
